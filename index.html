Lesson 1

How a language model learns

Start with one word. End with the only hardware question that matters: who holds the numbers.

A word

A word is a list of numbers.

The model cannot read letters. It turns each word into a vector, a list of heights. A real model uses thousands of numbers. Four is enough to see the idea.

The guess

It guesses the next word.

It does not look up an answer. It gives every possible next word a chance. The real next word is the one that was written. This picture is a toy, not a live model.

The miss

The miss is called the loss.

It is how surprised the model was by the real next word. It is not the gap between two word-numbers, and it is not that gap squared.

loss = −log(chance of the real next word)

Sure and right: the chance is near 1, and the loss is near 0. Sure and wrong: the chance is tiny, and the loss is large.

Training

Take a small step downhill.

You never solve for a slope of zero. There are too many inner numbers. Each example moves them a little. The loss never hits zero, because the next word is often unsure.

new = old − step × slope

The machine

Those numbers have to sit in memory.

While the model answers, the inner numbers live on a GPU. One card holds a smaller open model. A frontier-size model needs many cards.

One card

A smaller open model. This is the cheap test, then maybe the box you own.

Many cards

A frontier open model. A big purchase, and a datacenter, not a laptop.

Their cards

You call an API. You do not hold the numbers. They do.

The choice

Who can see the prompt, and who can switch it off.

API

Best quality today. Ordinary APIs can keep the prompt, and they can cut you off.

Rent

Same open model as buying. The host can see the box and can turn it off. Fine for fake data only.

Buy

The prompt stays on a machine you own. Harder to set up. This is the one that meets both rules.

Order: no one else keeps the data, then no one can cut you off, then quality, then cost.

Next

One task. Fake data. Then stop.

Pick one cron where the cheap model was already close. Write a mock with no client names or numbers. Run one open model on a rented card for a few hours, score it blind, and turn the machine off.

If it gets near the middle model, talk to the infra advisor about one card you own. Leave the wide gaps on the paid model. Do not buy a pile of cards for everything.
